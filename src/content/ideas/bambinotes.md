## The problem

For parents of preschool children, the school day is a black box: updates arrive through scattered calls, paper notes, and rushed pickup conversations, leaving parents disconnected from their children's early development. In the Arab world there was no Arabic-first tool built for how regional nurseries actually communicate.

## How it works

Bambinotes gives teachers and preschools a multi-language cloud platform to publish children's updates and reports, while parents follow everything through the same web-based platform or mobile applications. The goal, in founder Odeh Awwad's words, is to invigorate communication between parents and early-childhood institutions — daycares, preschools, and kindergartens — with a state-of-the-art platform that helps parents track and monitor their children's development.

## Pain points

Fragmented parent-teacher channels, parents missing early-development milestones, nurseries with no purpose-built software, and a regional market where the first comprehensive platform of this kind had to be built in Arabic from scratch.

## Business model

B2B software sold to early-childhood institutions: preschools, daycares, and kindergartens adopt the platform to offer parents a competitive, easy-to-use communication service and deeper involvement in their children's education.

## Challenges

Convincing thinly-resourced nurseries in three countries to pay for software, keeping the product current across languages and school workflows, and converting an undisclosed seed round into durable region-wide adoption.

## Funding

- Raised: undisclosed seed investment from Palestinian VC Ibtikar Fund (Jan 2018). Ibtikar's Managing General Partner Habib Hazzan called Bambinotes the first of its kind in the region in comprehensiveness, up-to-date technology, and ease of use — and the first platform available in Arabic (StartupScene; MENAbytes).
- History: originally started in 2012 and expanded to MENA in March 2017, run as an independent company led by Odeh Awwad (MENAbytes).

## Latest — Jan 2018

Bambinotes raised its seed round from Ibtikar Fund while operating across Palestine, Jordan, and Saudi Arabia — positioning an Arabic-first parent-teacher platform for wider regional rollout.
