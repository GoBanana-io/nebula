## The problem

For a brand like Coca-Cola or Nestle, the shelf is the moment of truth — but headquarters could not see it. Manual audits covered a fraction of stores and arrived weeks late, so out-of-stocks, missing promotions and broken planograms silently ate sales every day.

## How it works

Trax sends computer vision down the store aisle. Photos taken by field reps or fixed cameras are processed into SKU-level reads: what is in stock, what share of shelf each brand holds, whether the agreed display is actually up, and at what price. Dashboards turn that into corrective tasks while acquisitions — Qopius for real-time shelf monitoring, Planorama for European image recognition, Shopkick for shopper rewards data, Nielsen's store-observation assets — widened the data moat.

## Pain points

Empty shelves nobody reports, promotional displays that never get built, planogram compliance near zero in far-flung outlets, and audit reports so stale they describe a store that no longer exists.

## Business model

Enterprise SaaS: annual subscriptions and analytics licenses per brand or retailer, complemented by project-based in-store data collection and activation products that monetize the shopper side.

## Challenges

Long enterprise sales cycles with lumpy mega-contracts, direct rivalry from other shelf-AI vendors, and the strategic cost of breadth: in 2025 Trax divested its image-recognition business to Gemspring Capital, and in 2026 that unit merged with FORM — a reminder that even category pioneers must restructure to keep scaling.

## Funding

- Raised: $100M Series D led by HOPU Investments (Jul 2019) at a valuation over $1.1B, making Trax Singapore's second unicorn; total equity financing topped $350M with Warburg Pincus as largest shareholder alongside Boyu Capital, Investec and GIC (Retail Technology Innovation Hub).
- Valuation: over $1.1B at Series D (Jul 2019).

## Latest — February 2026

Gemspring Capital merged FORM (GoSpotCheck, FORM OpX) with Trax's image-recognition business to create a combined retail-execution and shelf-intelligence platform serving 750+ customers worldwide under FORM CEO Ali Moosani (PR Newswire via Third News) — the transaction that repositioned Trax Ltd.'s remaining businesses toward growth and market expansion.
