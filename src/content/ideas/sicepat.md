## The problem

Indonesia's e-commerce boom scattered millions of sellers across thousands of islands, but parcels moved on slow, fragmented couriers with no fulfillment backbone — a small merchant in Sulawesi had no way to promise Jakarta-speed delivery.

## How it works

Founded in 2014 by The Kim Hai under parent company Onstar Express, SiCepat expanded aggressively from 2018 with its SiCepat Ekspres last-mile subsidiary, then added fulfillment and warehouse management (HaiStar), air cargo (CKL Kargo), e-commerce enablers (Hera, Clodeo) and a joint venture with NFC Indonesia for electric delivery motorcycles — positioning as an end-to-end partner from online shop setup to doorstep delivery for MSMEs.

## Pain points

Late and lost inter-island parcels, sellers storing stock at home for lack of warehouses, no delivery SLA a micro-merchant could afford, and marketplaces needing one logistics partner covering first mile to last mile.

## Business model

Per-parcel courier fees on SiCepat Ekspres volumes, recurring fulfillment and warehousing contracts via HaiStar, air-cargo charges through CKL, plus enabler and software services sold to online sellers.

## Challenges

The courier price war with J&T Express keeps per-parcel margins wafer-thin; when pandemic demand cooled, SiCepat was swept into Indonesia's late-2022 retrenchment wave; and IPO ambitions — once framed around a $2B IDX listing — quietly faded as markets turned.

## Funding

- Raised: $50M Series A (April 2019), then a $170M Series B (March 2021) led across Falcon House Partners, Kejora Capital, DEG, MDI Ventures, Indies Capital, Pavilion Capital, Trihill Capital and Daiwa Securities — described at the time as Southeast Asia's largest Series B (TechCrunch, March 2021).
- Valuation: private company — no verified valuation disclosed.

## Latest — November 2022

SiCepat was named among roughly 16 Indonesian digital companies — alongside GoTo, Shopee, Xendit, Tanihub and Zenius — that carried out mass layoffs as the sector pivoted from growth to profitability (Tech Wire Asia, November 11, 2022, citing Bisnis.com).
