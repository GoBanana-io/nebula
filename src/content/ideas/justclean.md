## The problem

Laundry across the Gulf is a huge but stubbornly offline trade: customers juggling phone orders and counter drop-offs, and cleaners with no booking software, no delivery fleet, and no online demand.

## How it works

JustClean's app lets users schedule laundry and dry-cleaning pickup with doorstep return through a network of partner cleaners, with logistics coordinated on its platform. On the supply side, cleaners manage daily schedules, availability, and sales reporting through JustClean Business tooling — SaaS plus delivery support that turns independent shops into a managed network.

## Pain points

Untracked orders, rigid shop hours, variable quality, and for owners, missed calls and no data on their own business.

## Business model

Order flow across consumer and business customers, monetised alongside SaaS subscriptions and logistics services for cleaners — then extended into car wash (at-home or station) across the UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, and KSA.

## Challenges

Low average order values make every pickup kilometre count; service quality rests with independent partners; and each new country and vertical (car wash, deeper B2B) adds operational weight against regional rivals.

## Latest — March 2022

In March 2022 JustClean raised $6 million led by GIC to expand cleaning services across the GCC and deepen its SaaS platform, coinciding with the launch of its car-wash vertical in the UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, and KSA with a Qatar rollout to follow — positioning JustClean as the top marketplace, SaaS, and logistics provider for the region's cleaning industry.
