## The problem

Indonesia's best teachers and tutoring centers sit in Jakarta and a few other cities, while tens of millions of students live where teacher distribution is uneven and facilities lag. Private tutoring worked — but only for families who could afford it and reach it.

## How it works

Founded in 2014 by Adamas Belva Syah Devara and Iman Usman, Ruangguru built a mobile-first learning ecosystem: subscription video libraries, live tutor classes, standardised-test mocks and the Skill Academy for vocational courses, spanning 100+ subjects. It expanded to Vietnam in 2019 and Thailand (as StartDee) in 2020, and became a training partner of Indonesia's pre-employment card programme, putting its courses in front of job seekers nationwide.

## Pain points

Costly private tutoring, patchy rural teaching quality, exam seasons with no structured revision path, and adult workers with no affordable route to reskill.

## Business model

Paid consumer subscriptions for K-12 content, fees for vocational and corporate courses, and government-funded seats through workforce-training partnerships.

## Challenges

Free AI tutors and school platforms erode paid demand; proving learning gains across 22 million students is difficult; and Vietnam and Thailand each require fully localised curricula and teaching staff.
