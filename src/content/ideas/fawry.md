## The problem

In a cash-dominated economy, paying utility bills, topping up phones, or settling fees meant queues, cash handling, and no receipts — exclusionary for anyone far from a bank branch.

## Why it worked

Fawry recruited hundreds of thousands of small retailers as payment agents: customers hand cash to a shopkeeper, the shopkeeper settles digitally through Fawry. That physical on-ramp scaled to 29M+ monthly users and 250+ e-payment services, carrying the company to an EGX listing in August 2019 — Egypt's first fintech IPO — and later into microfinance via Fawry MSME Finance.

## Challenges

As a listed company every quarter is judged; bank-led wallets (InstaPay and others) and telecom money compete for the same transactions; and newer lending products add credit risk to a transaction-fee business.
