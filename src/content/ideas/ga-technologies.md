## The problem

Buying an investment condo in Japan meant station-front broker offices, paper contracts, and prices only insiders could judge — while the brokerages themselves ran leasing paperwork by hand.

## How it works

RENOSY, the flagship app, matches buyers with AI-priced investment condos, closes contracts digitally, and manages the units afterward with resale built in. Beside it, the ITANDI suite (BB marketplace, rental management, brokerage and buy-sell tools) sells workflow software to the industry, and spica consulting applies the playbook to M&A deals.

## Pain points

Price opacity for newcomers, closing friction, absentee-landlord management headaches, and brokerages buried in manual admin.

## Business model

Sales margins on investment properties at national scale plus recurring SaaS subscriptions from brokerages — a transaction-plus-software stack on the same asset base.

## Challenges

Real-estate margins bite when costs rise, earnings wobble with deal timing, and the rebranded US arm (RENOSY by Renters Warehouse) still has to earn its place.
