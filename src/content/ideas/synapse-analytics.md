## The idea

Regulated lenders juggle separate vendors for onboarding, credit scoring, fraud, AML, and collections — black boxes they neither own nor fully control, slowing every decision. Synapse Analytics sells one agentic, AI-native platform that lets banks own their decisioning outright across the whole credit lifecycle.

## How it works

- Connects to bank data across products and channels.
- AI agents score, approve, flag, and route — with the bank holding the controls, not the vendor.
- Covers the lifecycle end to end: onboarding → credit → fraud/AML → collections.
- Proof points do the selling: $200M+ in lending supported, NPLs cut by up to 40% at client banks.

## Who pays

Enterprise platform fees plus implementation and expansion modules. Land with one lifecycle stage, expand to all five.

## Why it worked

Ownership is the differentiator in a regulated market. Banks don't just buy accuracy — they buy control over the models that decide who gets money.

## Challenges

Enterprise bank deals take quarters to close; GCC and international expansion tests a Cairo-built team; incumbents like FICO plus funded AI startups contest every RFP.

## Steal this

If you sell to regulated incumbents, don't pitch AI magic — pitch a controlled experiment on their ugliest queue (collections, fraud alerts), publish the before/after, and let procurement pull you inward.
