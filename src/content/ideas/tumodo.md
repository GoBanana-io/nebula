## The problem

Business travel at regional companies ran on emails, offline agencies, and messy expense claims: finance had no control over what employees booked, trips cost more than they should, and nobody could see the full travel budget in one place.

## How it works

Tumodo gives companies an online business-travel platform where teams book flights and hotels — even for large numbers of employees — within about two minutes. Inventory is aggregated from more than 400 airlines and over 2 million accommodation options; company travel policies are enforced at booking time; and dashboards track spend, delivering average savings of 35% on travel expenses.

## Pain points

Slow manual approvals, agency markups with no transparency, off-policy bookings, scattered invoicing, and no consolidated view of travel spend or traveller whereabouts.

## Business model

A B2B travel-management marketplace: Tumodo earns on the corporate bookings flowing through its platform and grows distribution through employer-facing partnerships such as its Tabby integration for travel management.

## Challenges

Living up to a $35M pre-seed — an unusually large round for the stage — demands very fast scaling; the stated goal of reaching 25 additional countries by 2026 stretches operations, localisation, and support; and entrenched agencies plus corporate inertia make enterprise switching slow.

## Funding

- Raised: $35M pre-seed (Jan 2024), co-led by MENA-focused angel investors, earmarked for UAE presence, product development, and MENA partnerships (StartupScene, Jan 2024).

## Latest — June 2024

In June 2024 Tumodo partnered with Saudi fintech Tabby to enhance travel management — integrating its services into Tabby's travel-management system for planning business trips — a step in its push to digitise business travel across the UAE and GCC with AI-enabled tooling.
