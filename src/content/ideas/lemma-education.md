## The problem

One-to-one maths tutoring works, but a human tutor costs roughly $30 an hour — far beyond what most families can pay regularly, leaving students to struggle alone or fall behind.

## How it works

Lemma Education is an AI-powered maths tutor that listens to students in real time and watches them solve problems on a digital canvas, responding the way a human tutor would. Because early research showed students learn very differently from one another, the platform accepts voice, typing, and handwriting input at the same time, so each learner can work in whatever mode suits them.

## Pain points

Expensive hourly tutoring, static worksheets that cannot see where a student goes wrong, and classrooms too crowded for teachers to adapt to each child's unique learning style.

## Business model

No public pricing yet — at incubator stage the team is building the tutor, not monetising it. The natural path is a per-student subscription sold to families, or school licences sold across the region's private-school market.

## Challenges

Still at incubator stage with unproven learning outcomes and no disclosed paying users; the team must show the tutor teaches better than the free general-purpose AI chatbots families already use, and win the trust of parents and schools on student data.
