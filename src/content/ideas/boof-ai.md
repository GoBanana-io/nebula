## The problem

International sanctions led global AI platforms to restrict Iranian users and businesses, and foreign models perform weakly in Persian — leaving Iranians with no capable AI assistant in their own language and firms with nowhere safe to send their data.

## How it works

An Iranian knowledge-based company built Boof, a native Persian AI engine served under the Boof AI brand through apps and a tools gallery. Users chat in Persian, generate and process images, summarise files with interactive Q&A, and get programming help plus advisory answers in fields such as healthcare and education. A trial version is open to the public while a professional version targets organisations and businesses; the company's own site details text-to-speech and speech-to-text services trained on over 6,000 hours of audio data alongside its language models.

## Pain points

Blocked access to ChatGPT-class tools, poor Persian quality from outside models, enterprise fear of hosting sensitive data abroad, and dollar-priced subscriptions out of reach for most Iranian users.

## Business model

Freemium SaaS: a free public trial builds the user base while institutions pay for the professional tier spanning chat, voice, image and developer-assistant services.

## Challenges

A young team racing frontier global models with limited compute, the burden of proving its Persian-superiority claim to sceptical users, converting free users into paying organisations, and financing training with no access to foreign venture capital.
