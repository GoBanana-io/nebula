## The problem

Marathon gaming and desk sessions punished backs, yet the market offered either generic office chairs or flimsy lookalike racing seats with no real ergonomics.

## How it works

Founded in 2014 by former pro gamers Ian Ang and Alaric Choo, Secretlab designs its TITAN Evo chairs, MAGNUS desks and accessories in-house at its 44,000 sq ft Braddell global HQ and R&D centre, manufactures at scale (about a million chairs a year), and sells direct-to-consumer across some 60 countries — amplified by licensed editions with major game studios and entertainment franchises.

## Pain points

Chronic back and neck pain from long sessions, chairs that sagged within months, and office furniture that ignored gaming culture entirely.

## Business model

Premium DTC hardware margins, higher-priced collaboration editions, and accessories and desk attach that raise average order value.

## Challenges

Demand cooled after the pandemic home-office surge; copycat brands undercut on price; and Temasek-linked minority backing (Heliconia, 2019) sets a high bar for the next phase of growth beyond chairs.
