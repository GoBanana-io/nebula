## The problem

Chip and systems giants invest a year just training a new design engineer, yet cannot find enough of them — while Palestinian universities graduate some 3,000 computer scientists a year into an economy with 21 percent unemployment, separated from those employers by permits and borders (IEEE Spectrum, 2009).

## How it works

Asal recruits and vets Palestinian engineers into dedicated pods housed in Ramallah — and in the planned city of Rawabi — that operate as long-term extensions of the client's R&D org. Apple's Rawabi center, staffed entirely through Asal, works with teams in Herzliya and Haifa on hardware-development tools and products; other pods serve Microsoft (including Surface pen-and-touch R&D), Intel, Mellanox-Nvidia, and LivePerson. G.ho.st's CEO pointed chip-design firm Nuvoton to Asal's Murad Tahboub precisely because the firm could locate every engineer it needed (IEEE Spectrum).

## Pain points

Costly hiring mistakes after year-long training ramps, acute shortages of specialized hardware talent, and graduates whose English, shared time zone, and lower wages make them ideal for outsourcing — if a trusted intermediary bridges the access gap.

## Business model

Contract R&D outsourcing on multi-year engagements: clients pay per dedicated engineer or pod while Asal carries recruitment, facilities, management, and retention risk.

## Challenges

Concentration in a few giant clients whose roadmap shifts can idle whole pods, retention of senior hardware engineers against emigration offers, and growing headcount under movement restrictions that make client-site visits a permit-dependent exception rather than the norm.

## Funding

No public record of venture funding — Asal grew as a services exporter on multinational contracts, operating since at least 2009 (IEEE Spectrum). It is associated with the Massar ecosystem: Rawabi founder Bashar Masri sits on Asal's board (Algemeiner, Jul 2022).

## Latest — Jul 2022

Apple announced the expansion of its Palestinian R&D center in Rawabi, which already employed 60 engineers — all recruited through Ramallah-based contractor Asal Technologies, starting with the first five in 2018 — working with teams in Herzliya and Haifa on hardware products (Algemeiner, Jul 2022).
