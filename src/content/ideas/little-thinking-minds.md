## The problem

Millions of Arabic-speaking children learn to read with content translated from English or none at all — while schools and ministries across MENA had no evidence-based digital curricula built for Arabic literacy from the ground up.

## How it works

Little Thinking Minds builds Arabic literacy programmes — I Read Arabic, I Start Arabic, and the Mizan Assessment Platform — that schools, ministries, and school groups license to teach reading with culturally relevant material and track progress through assessments. Under Seesaw, these curricula plug into interactive PreK-12 tools, AI-driven practice, and dashboards that give educators and ministries data on student progress.

## Pain points

Poor early-Arabic reading outcomes, Western content that misses Arab cultural and linguistic realities, teachers without measurement tools, and education ministries hunting for home-grown digital curricula.

## Business model

Institutional licensing: schools, ministries, and school groups pay for the literacy platforms and assessments — a B2B engine that Seesaw can now carry into its 25-million-educator global network.

## Challenges

The test of the merger is outcome data: showing Arabic-literacy gains inside Seesaw's platform, shipping Seesaw's first Arabic-language platform, and keeping ministry contracts steady while two product cultures integrate.

## Funding

- Raised: $1.765M Series A closed May 2019 — a $500k tranche from ISSF (its first disclosed investment) topping a $1.265M first tranche led by Algebra Ventures with Mindshift Capital and Al Turki Group (Wamda). At the time the platforms served 200+ schools and 100,000 students.
- Exit: acquired by Seesaw for an undisclosed value, announced 15 April 2025 (Wamda).

## Latest — Apr 2025

On 15 April 2025 US PreK-12 platform Seesaw announced its acquisition of Little Thinking Minds for an undisclosed value, combining Seesaw's interactive technology with the company's evidence-based Arabic literacy curricula and launching Seesaw's push into its first Arabic-language platform (Wamda; Arageek).
