## The problem

In MENA, launching a payment card meant a months-long slog: find a processor, secure a BIN sponsor with card-scheme membership, and pay legacy vendors whose licensed, third-party technology was expensive, slow, and missing the modern APIs fintechs needed.

## How it works

NymCard offers plug-and-play card infrastructure on its proprietary nCore platform: full-stack, API-first issuing and processing, embedded lending, and real-time money movement. Clients — banks, fintechs, enterprises, telecom operators — launch branded virtual and plastic card programs with mobile wallets in about eight weeks, while NymCard holds the regulated ground (in-principle approval from ADGM's Financial Services Regulatory Authority to offer money services).

## Pain points

BIN-sponsorship bottlenecks, card launches measured in months, legacy stacks with no usable APIs, and founders burning runway on payments compliance instead of product.

## Business model

B2B infrastructure: fees from card programs issued and processed on nCore, plus embedded-lending and money-movement services, sold to financial institutions and large enterprises across the region.

## Challenges

Card issuance is tightly controlled and highly regulated in every market, so each of the 10+ MENA countries adds licensing complexity; global Banking-as-a-Service entrants are circling the same clients; and growth across three verticals strains program-management capacity.
