## The problem

Distributors of fast-moving consumer goods and pharmaceuticals live or die by their field reps — yet most manage them blind: visits happen off-system, orders arrive by phone or WhatsApp, and nobody can verify shelf execution. International CRM tools are built for desk salespeople and rarely speak Arabic, so adoption in the field stalls.

## How it works

Repzo is a mobile-first sales force automation suite. Reps log geo-tagged activities (orders, payments, merchandising checks) from an Android/iOS app; managers see live GPS movement, KPI dashboards, and inventory positions. Integrations with SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, QuickBooks, Xero, and local ERPs pipe field data straight into the back office, and the company has extended the same tracking model to field-service maintenance work.

## Pain points

Unverifiable rep KPIs, manual ordering over calls and messages, stock blind spots between warehouse and shelf, and English-only software that Arabic-speaking teams won't use.

## Business model

B2B SaaS: distributors and brand owners pay per field seat for tracking, ordering, inventory, and merchandising modules, with accounts expanding as clients add reps, subsidiaries, and countries.

## Challenges

Enterprise distribution sales cycles are long and the Riyadh expansion adds office and hiring costs; meanwhile global CRM suites and regional SFA startups chase the same FMCG pitch, so Repzo's moat is its field-specific depth and ERP integrations rather than price.
