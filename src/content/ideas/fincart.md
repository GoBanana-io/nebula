## The idea

Egyptian social and marketplace sellers lose orders to courier failures, cash-on-delivery delays, and fragmented tools — shipping in one app, chat in another, financing nowhere. Fincart collapses it all into one AI-powered operating platform: route the parcel, engage the buyer, advance the cash.

## How it works

- Connects 40+ couriers across Africa into a single routing interface with AI selection.
- Adds customer-engagement tooling so sellers recover failed or COD orders.
- Layers cash advances underwritten on the seller's own fulfilment data.

## Who pays

SMB subscriptions and orchestration fees, plus financing margin on advances — logistics software that earns like a lender.

## Why it worked

It owned the workflow before owning the assets. Couriers are interchangeable; the dashboard with the seller's history isn't.

## Challenges

Delivery quality depends on third-party couriers; SMB churn is high when volumes dip; each new MENA/African market adds courier integrations, payments, and support overhead.

## Steal this

Find merchants living in spreadsheets and WhatsApp, give them one screen that removes today's fire (shipping), then sell them tomorrow's fuel (cash) priced on the data today's screen generated.
