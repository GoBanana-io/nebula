## The problem

Egyptian social and marketplace sellers lose orders to courier failures, cash-on-delivery delays and fragmented tools — shipping in one app, chat in another, financing nowhere.

## How it works

Founded in Cairo in 2023 by Mostafa Masry and Nihal Ali, Fincart began by connecting merchants to last-mile providers and evolved into an AI-powered operating system: one dashboard integrating 40+ courier companies across Africa, reconciling COD payments, running agentic tools for retention and engagement, and extending short-term cash advances based on sales.

## Pain points

Merchants juggled a myriad of software for shipping, customer support, marketing and payments, with unreliable last-mile delivery and COD reconciliation pain that kept e-commerce SMBs from scaling.

## Business model

E-commerce infrastructure monetized through logistics orchestration across one of Africa's largest courier-integration networks, plus embedded finance via sales-based cash advances.

## Why it worked

Founded in 2023 by Mostafa Masry and Nihal Ali, Fincart integrated 40+ courier companies across Africa into a single AI-powered operating platform covering shipping, customer engagement and cash advances. Merchants manage fulfilment from one dashboard while Fincart monetizes logistics orchestration and embedded finance.

## Challenges

Delivery quality depends on third-party couriers; SMB churn is high when volumes dip; and each new MENA/African market adds courier integrations, payments and support overhead.

Fincart closed its oversubscribed $2.8M seed round (co-led by Launch Africa and Antler MENAP) to deepen its AI platform, expand the team and push across MENA and Africa — reporting 450+ merchants and nearly EGP 1B (~$20M) in processed merchandise value, with expansion beyond Egypt planned from 2027.
