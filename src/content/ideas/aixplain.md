## The problem

Most companies could see that AI was the answer to their business problems but had no idea where to start. The technology was trapped behind a historic skills gap: a new generation of AI practitioners was emerging on every continent, yet only the biggest tech companies could find them, hire them, or put their skills to use.

## How it works

Founded in 2020 by Syrian-American machine-learning veteran Hassan Sawaf — who previously headed AI divisions at eBay, Amazon Web Services and Facebook — aiXplain is a collaborative marketplace for AI development. Customers turn pre-made datasets and pre-vetted models into custom solutions without writing code, or team up with AI creators; suppliers of datasets and models sell to them and use built-in benchmarking tools to improve their offerings. Its "super agent" Bel Esprit lets non-technical users describe what they need in plain language and get working AI apps and agents, with a model marketplace and evaluation system to pick the right model per use case. Almost half of its customers are based in the Middle East.

## Pain points

AI locked behind expertise; executives unable to reach AI talent; no practical path from chatbot experiments to agents that take action — and most platforms under-serving Arabic and its dialects.

## Business model

Pay-as-you-go AI development: users pay to build, benchmark and deploy solutions, while the platform takes its cut connecting enterprise demand with creators and suppliers of models, datasets and pipelines.

## Challenges

Hyperscalers and a stampede of agent startups are racing the same opportunity; real-world agent reliability is still unproven; and expanding across regions with very different AI readiness takes sustained enterprise selling.

## Funding

- Raised: $6.5M pre-Series A led by Wa'ed Ventures, Aramco's venture arm — its first US AI investment — bringing total funding to $16.5M (Jul 2024). The company was already profitable the previous year.
- Previously: an $8M round in 2023 and an early round led by Transform VC after more than a year in stealth, with a private beta from 2021.

## Latest — July 2024

On 17 July 2024 Axios reported Wa'ed Ventures' $6.5M pre-Series A into San Francisco-based aiXplain to expand operations and push generative AI from chatbots into action-taking agents with better Arabic representation.
