## The problem

Delivery across MENA still runs on manual work: dispatchers assign orders by phone, nobody sees drivers live, cash-on-delivery money leaks through slow reconciliation, and support teams drown in "where is my order" messages — costs couriers and merchants absorb on every route.

## How it works

Olivery offers an end-to-end, low-code/no-code delivery platform. Couriers, retailers, and e-commerce brands consolidate every step — order creation, automated routing, driver tracking, cash-on-delivery reconciliation, and customer notifications — into one dashboard that non-technical ops teams configure themselves, cutting costs and delivery times while lifting customer satisfaction.

## Pain points

Spreadsheet dispatch, invisible drivers, COD cash gaps, repetitive status inquiries, and delivery software that needs engineers the courier doesn't have.

## Business model

Subscription SaaS for delivery operations: logistics providers and merchants pay recurring fees to run on Olivery, with the roadmap adding AI-powered predictive routing, automated data entry, and proactive customer support.

## Challenges

Operating in nine countries multiplies integration and onboarding work across fragmented courier ecosystems; and bigger logistics suites plus couriers' own in-house tools compete on price for the same dispatch workflows.

## Funding

- Raised: undisclosed joint seed investment (Jul 2025) from Ibtikar Fund and Flat6Labs Mashreq Seed Fund — Flat6Labs' first institutional backing followed on in the Ibtikar-led round — earmarked for AI features, regional expansion, and customer onboarding.

## Latest — Jul 2025

In July 2025 Olivery announced its joint seed investment from Ibtikar Fund and Flat6Labs Mashreq Seed Fund to scale its no-code delivery platform across MENA, serving 200+ active clients in nine countries while launching AI-powered routing and support features.
