## The problem

For amateur footballers in Kuwait, getting a game meant a nightly scramble: messaging groups, calling pitches, and hoping a slot was free — while facility owners watched peak evening hours go unbooked for lack of any storefront.

## How it works

Li3ib is an app and website for sports venue reservations: users find nearby facilities, book pitches, schedule recreational games with other players, and set up their own tournaments, including managed competitions for companies. Beyond bookings, the company handles facility upkeep coordination and event organisation, promoting sports activity at multiple levels.

## Pain points

Group-chat chaos to assemble a game, double-booked or locked pitches on arrival, and newcomers with no route into a match without a personal invite.

## Business model

A cut of facility bookings plus fees for organising tournaments and running corporate competitions.

## Challenges

Keeping both sides liquid — pitches listed and players showing up at the same hours — charging for bookings without pushing casual players back to free chats, and growing past a small domestic market.
