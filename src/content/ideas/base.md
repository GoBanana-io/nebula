## The problem

Japan's small shopkeepers watched e-commerce grow from the sidelines: hiring someone to build a store cost money they didn't have, and the tools assumed skills they didn't possess.

## How it works

BASE's hosted builder asks for three fields and produces a working storefront. Around it sit the money rails: PAY.JP for online card payments, the PAY ID buyer app, YELL BANK advances against future sales, and want.jp for cross-border selling — so a hobby seller can graduate to a real business without switching platforms.

## Pain points

Zero technical skills, zero budget, no way to accept cards online, and no collateral for a bank loan when stock needs funding.

## Business model

Stores are free; BASE earns a cut of transactions plus payment-processing and financing income, betting that group GMV scale — about ¥500B a year after E-Store joined in 2025 — compounds take-rate revenue.

## Challenges

Monetising free users without triggering churn, sustaining growth after the pandemic pull-forward faded, and holding off global builders and domestic mall giants courting the same sellers.
