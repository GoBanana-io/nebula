## The idea

Egyptian online sellers lived or died on couriers they couldn't track: parcels lost, cash-on-delivery uncollected, customers blaming the merchant for the courier's failures. Founded in 2017 by Mohamed Ezzat and Ahmed Gaber, Bosta built delivery infrastructure for e-commerce first — next-day service, live tracking, cash collection — rather than retrofitting a traditional courier, then expanded into Saudi Arabia.

## How it works

- Merchant hands over parcels; Bosta sorts (including its $5M automated facility), routes, and dispatches next-day.
- Every parcel is tracked live; COD cash is collected and reconciled back to the seller.
- Warehouses (~50) and riders (~8,000) are sized for e-commerce peaks, with an estimated ~20% of domestic parcel volume.

## Who pays

Per-shipment fees plus handling, returns, and storage. Density per lane is the profit engine.

## Why it worked

It sold peace of mind, not transport. Tracking plus guaranteed cash return removed the two existential merchant fears — and merchants consolidated volume onto the reliable option.

## Challenges

Diesel and FX swings hit every lane; quality across thousands of riders is a permanent grind; a public listing puts quarterly scrutiny on thin delivery margins.

## Steal this

In any market where sellers complain about couriers, don't compete on price — compete on reconciliation: guaranteed tracking plus guaranteed cash-back dates. Win the sellers' trust ledger first.

## Traction signal (context, not the story)

- Roughly $27M raised since launch with a $32M round alongside the IPO track (EnterpriseAM, Feb 2026); $6.7M Series A led by Silicon Badia (2021).

## الفكرة بالعربي

**الفكرة:** لا تنافس شركات الشحن على السعر — ابنِ ما يتمناه بائع الإنترنت: توصيل اليوم التالي بتتبع حي وتحصيل نقدي مضمون الإرجاع.

**كيف تعمل:** يستلم ويفرز ويوجّه الطرود عبر مخازن ومركز فرز آلي، ويوصّل في اليوم التالي مع تتبع وتحصيل وتسوية نقدية للتاجر.

**من يدفع:** التاجر عن كل شحنة والخدمات المضافة؛ والكثافة على المسار هي محرك الربح.

**كيف تسرقها:** في أي سوق يشتكي فيها الباعة من الشحن، نافس على التسوية المضمونة (تتبع + موعد استرداد نقدي ثابت) لا على السعر. اكسب دفتر ثقة البائعين أولًا.
