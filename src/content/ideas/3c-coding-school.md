## The idea

Parents across MENA want children prepared for an AI economy, but school computer classes teach memorization, not problem-solving, and private courses vary wildly in quality. 3C's answer is a real curriculum: leveled, project-based, and visibly progressive — from first code to AI, game dev, and cybersecurity.

## How it works

- Leveled tracks take kids from basics to building (games, models, secure code) with portfolios, not certificates.
- Physical branches deliver the high-touch teaching; online extends reach beyond them.
- Instructor training plus a (promised) adaptive AI layer keep quality from depending on one star teacher.

## Who pays

Parents per course or subscription; franchisees for the system; eventually schools buying the curriculum wholesale.

## Why it worked

It sold progress parents can see: a child who demos a game or a model renews. 120,000+ students and 230% revenue growth say the ladder resonates.

## Challenges

Youth education is instructor-dependent; Saudi entry tests hiring, localization, and unit economics; the AI-personalized platform must adapt to each student's pace without replacing teachers.

## Steal this

Pick any parent-anxiety skill and build the visible ladder: levels, demo days, portfolios. Distribution through branches or schools beats pure online when the buyer (parent) needs to trust the teacher.

**الفكرة:** مسار منظّم يأخذ الطفل من أول سطر إلى محو أمية ذكاء حقيقية — بمشاريع يعرضها لا شهادات يحفظها.

**كيف تعمل:** مسارات متدرجة في الفروع وعبر الإنترنت، وتدريب معلمين وطبقة ذكاء تكيّفية للحفاظ على الجودة.

**من يدفع:** الأهل اشتراكات، والفروع رسوم منظومة، والمدارس لاحقًا بالجملة.

**كيف تسرقها:** اختر أي مهارة تقلق الأهل وابنِ السلّم المرئي: مستويات وأيام عرض ومحافظ أعمال. التقدّم الذي يراه الأب هو ما يجدّد.
