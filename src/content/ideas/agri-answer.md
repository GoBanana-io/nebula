## The problem

Across Syria and its refugee farming communities, smallholders make high-stakes calls — what to plant, when to spray, how to water — without an agronomist to ask. Extension services are thin, private advice is priced for agribusiness, and a wrong guess costs a season.

## How it works

Founded by Masaoud Ali and Doaa Al-Ibrahim, Agri-Answer is an agricultural company concept that connects farmers through the internet and its applications to answer their questions and help them manage their crops. The pitch, in Ali's words: the best solution at the least cost, delivered as a seasonal subscription timed to the growing calendar.

## Pain points

Advice arrives late or not at all, field visits cost more than the diagnosis is worth, and generic guidance misses the local pest, soil, and water picture — exactly where a subscription Q&A layer fits.

## Business model

Seasonal subscriptions: charge farmers once per growing season for ongoing answers and crop-management support, keeping the price low enough for smallholders while matching revenue to the agricultural cycle.

## Challenges

This is a paper-stage idea: despite winning its Idea Stage track, Agri-Answer never transitioned past incubation into a functional market tool. Reaching offline farmers, staffing agronomists per crop region, and converting free advice-seekers into paying subscribers were all unproven.

## Funding

- Raised: none — concept stage; the Erbil win brought a shot at up to $8,000 in cash prizes at the SPARK Ignite Conference finals in Amsterdam, not an investment round.
- Valuation: none.

## Latest — October 2018

On 4 October 2018 Agri-Answer won the Idea Stage track of the Jusoor–SPARK Startup Roadshow in Erbil, qualifying for the finals at the SPARK Ignite Conference in Amsterdam.
