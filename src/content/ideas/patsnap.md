## The problem

R&D is one of the largest line items in any technology company, yet decisions about it were made half-blind: patent filings lived in arcane databases, competitor moves surfaced years late, and nobody could say whether the innovation budget was buying anything defensible.

## How it works

PatSnap started in 2007 as a global patent-search database and grew into what co-founder Jeffrey Tiong calls "innovation intelligence": one platform combining 120M+ patents with scientific papers, grant records and funding news. AI search and analytics let R&D and IP teams run prior-art checks, map competitor technology landscapes, spot white space and identify acquisition or partnership targets. In China it operates as Zhihuiya, serving retail brands, research institutes, AI firms and pharma giants.

## Pain points

Duplicated research on already-patented ideas, rival filings discovered after launch, innovation treated as an unmeasurable KPI, and partner searches driven by conferences and guesswork instead of data.

## Business model

Seat-based enterprise SaaS for search plus premium intelligence modules, expanded into vertical datasets (biopharma) and distribution integrations (FactSet) that put PatSnap data in front of 10,000+ investment professionals.

## Challenges

Monetizing in China where companies resist Western SaaS pricing, defending premium search against free AI overviews and open search tools, and converting a 2021-vintage $1B+ valuation into a public-market exit after a long late-stage drought.

## Funding

- Raised: $300M Series E led by SoftBank Vision Fund 2 and Tencent (Mar 2021) with CITIC Industrial Fund, Sequoia China, Shunwei Capital, Vertex Growth and Vertex Ventures SEA & India; total raised passed $400M (TechCrunch).
- Valuation: crossed $1B at Series E (Mar 2021, per Tiong to TechCrunch).

## Latest — June 2026

PatSnap confidentially filed for a dual IPO in Hong Kong and Singapore (Bloomberg, via Asia Tech Review), reportedly targeting a $300–400M raise at a valuation above $2B — a potential landmark exit for Southeast Asian venture after the underwhelming Grab and GoTo listings.
