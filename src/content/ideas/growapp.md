## The problem

Working parents drop their children at the nursery gate and hear nothing until pickup, while nurseries coordinate attendance, activities, and parent questions through ad-hoc calls and messages that eat staff time.

## How it works

GrowApp is a childcare-management platform that bridges parents, nannies, and nursery administrators: activity tracking and real-time updates show parents their child's day moment by moment, direct channels connect parents with nannies, and organised schedules and activities structure the nursery's operations — framed by founders Safaa and Doaa as change starting from a single step.

## Pain points

No parental visibility during nursery hours, fragmented nanny-parent messaging, and manual administrative routines with no purpose-built local tool.

## Business model

Early-stage SaaS for nurseries: institutions adopt the system and onboard their parents (no pricing or revenue published yet).

## Challenges

Everything of an idea-stage venture: proving the product beyond its first partner nursery (Revano Nursery), earning parents' trust on children's data and safety, and securing funding — no investment has been announced.
